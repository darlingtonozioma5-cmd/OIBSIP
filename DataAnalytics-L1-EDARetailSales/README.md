# Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

## Oasis Infobyte Internship — Data Analytics

### Project Objective
To explore retail sales data and identify sales trends, customer demographics, product category performance and actionable business insights.

### Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

### Project Activities
- Dataset inspection and data quality assessment
- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Descriptive statistical analysis
- Monthly and quarterly sales analysis
- Customer age and gender analysis
- Product category performance analysis
- Correlation heatmap
- Data visualisation
- Business insights and recommendations

## Key Findings

- Total revenue across 400 transactions was ₦158,991,800.
- Rice 50kg was the highest-performing product, generating ₦71,959,300 in sales, with a recorded quantity of 837 units.
- Port Harcourt generated the highest regional sales at ₦55,167,300, followed by Owerri at ₦44,779,500.
- June recorded the highest monthly sales at ₦29,423,500, while May had the lowest at ₦23,867,700.
- Sales increased by approximately 23.28% from May to June.
- Vegetable Oil generated ₦13,436,700 in sales.
- The analysis showed that product unit prices contributed significantly to differences in total sales.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Prioritise high-performing products:** Maintain sufficient stock of Rice 50kg because it contributes significantly to overall revenue.

2. **Strengthen regional sales strategies:** Use the strong performance in Port Harcourt as a benchmark while developing targeted sales campaigns for regions with lower revenue.

3. **Prepare for seasonal demand:** Review the factors behind June's higher sales and plan inventory and promotional activities accordingly.

4. **Review product pricing:** Monitor unit prices and sales volumes to identify opportunities to improve revenue without negatively affecting demand.

5. **Monitor product performance:** Regularly track sales by product and region to identify changing customer preferences and improve inventory decisions.

### Dataset Limitation
The dataset contains product categories rather than individual product names. Therefore, product-level top-10 analysis is not possible with the available fields.
